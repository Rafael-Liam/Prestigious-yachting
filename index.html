The experience

A sunset cruise, shaped around you.

Rather than following a fixed route, your skipper reads the light and the sea as the evening moves, keeping you facing the best of the sky. It's a private outing from the first minute to the last — never shared with other guests, never rushed.

Departures usually fall between 5 and 7pm, adjusted to that evening's sunset and, where possible, to your own schedule.
